## Chris's observation notes

Watch without explaining every control. Record interventions, not just whether the task finished. If the tester is blocked, help and mark the intervention. Separate installation/provider sign-in time from model work. One friend's feedback helps find problems; it does not establish broad product demand. Track issues as blocker / confusing / suggestion, with an owner and status. Use the hosting questions only with people who express interest or describe a relevant need; treat demand as a hypothesis. Keep identifiable responses in a private record, never committed to this repository.
