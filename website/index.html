A THREAD THROUGH THE WORK

Start with an outcome.
Come back to a next step.

A useful conversation should leave something you can build on.

  1. 01 / SET THE GOAL

    Say what you want to achieve.

    Agree on success, where things stand and a concrete next action. Give the goal a named lead.

    “Help me ship the customer portal. Keep this as a lasting goal.”
  2. 02 / DO AND CHECK

    Take one useful step.

    Ask your agent to work. Substantial tasks have visible checks and results you can inspect, with decisions kept in the conversation.

    “Test the report download and show me what passed.”
  3. 03 / RETURN AND CONTINUE

    Pick up the same project.

    Reopen Abralo to find the goal, progress and next step. Correct the direction or ask the lead to continue.

    “Where did we get to, and what should we do next?”

You set the pace. Work starts from your requests or an explicit schedule. A lasting goal preserves the direction; it does not promise continuous background work or a guaranteed outcome.

THE WORKSPACE AROUND THE GOAL

People, agents and context.
In one place.

Your agents, together

Work with Codex, Claude or OpenCode through direct conversations and shared rooms, using your own supported provider account.

Memory with a purpose

Keep useful project context and decisions available for the next task. Private direct conversations stay private to their participants.

Progress you can inspect

See the current position, next action and evidence behind completed work. Keep talking while a task runs.

Local, with clear boundaries

Your workspace runs on your computer. Model requests go to your chosen provider. Local storage does not mean offline AI processing.
