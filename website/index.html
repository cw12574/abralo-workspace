What does Abralo actually do?

Your company's history is spread across conversations, documents, projects, tools and the AI chats your team has already had. Important context gets hard to find, so people repeat the same research and agents start with only the information someone remembered to paste in.

Abralo indexes that shared history and makes it searchable. When a person or agent needs context, they can look across the organisation's accumulated work instead of starting from a blank page. Private direct chats stay private.

A company’s memory grows with the work.

Every conversation, decision and project adds to the record people and agents can search. A new piece of work can connect to what the company already learned.

That means the next question can start with more of the story already in reach.

Beyond search.

Search is the beginning. We’re building toward a company memory that carries decisions forward, helps people and agents pick up work, and brings more of what happens in meetings into the record.
