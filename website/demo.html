What this experiment does—and what it measures

This is a seeded traffic toy, not a calibrated transport model. Both cities begin after the same 45-second warm-up and receive the same demand setting. Only your city receives bridge closures. Cars already on a bridge finish crossing; others replan at junctions. Closing all bridges can stop new trips being admitted, so fewer waiting cars does not necessarily mean a better result.

The original engine caps each city at 120 active cars. Capacity differences can change subsequent admitted trips. Counters include the warm-up; time above starts at the beginning of your experiment. Charts retain 300 simulated seconds; the export retains up to 200 interventions. Reset reproduces the initial state. The comparison, controls, chart and export were added by the launch operator to the team's original city. No agents or accounts run in your browser.
